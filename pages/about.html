01 / IDENTITY

More Than Just Code.

The person building things, and why it matters to them.

I'm Reabetswe — a third-year Computer Science student developing my skills through coursework, technical exploration, and hands-on projects. I'm passionate about understanding how software works at every level, from algorithms and data structures to networks and operating systems.

What draws me to technology is the combination of logic and creativity it requires. Writing code that actually solves a real problem — something a person can use — is genuinely satisfying. I find myself most energized when working through a problem that has multiple possible solutions.

I'm currently building practical skills in software development, refining my understanding of databases and networking, and working toward becoming a developer who builds things that are both functional and well-designed.

What I am currently learning: Deeper software engineering concepts, system design thinking, and applying algorithms to real scenarios beyond classroom exercises.

What I want to build: Software that solves real problems, is clean under the hood, and is genuinely useful to the people who use it.
